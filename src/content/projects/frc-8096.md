and robotics fundamentals to new members, delegated tasks across the sub-team by skill
level, reviewed pull requests, and wrote robot code in Java for both autonomous and
driver-controlled operations.

As Co-Captain, I coordinated build sessions and team events, and assisted with outreach
projects. At the three to four competitions we entered each year, I served as one of the
Pit Crew Leads, where I refined robot software between matches, ran system checks, and
assisted with mechanical issues. I also fielded questions from judges and other teams
about our team structure, outreach, and safety.
