I worked at the <a href="https://airlab.cs.uchicago.edu/" target="_blank" rel="noopener
noreferrer">Amyoli Internet Research Lab</a>, a human-computer interaction lab in the
University of Chicago's Department of Computer Science, for two summers.

During the first summer, I performed data analysis for a study on the quantitative effects
of dark patterns in subscription video-on-demand platforms. I ran statistical analyses and
built visualizations to explore potential findings with the first author, and wrote a
validation script to flag participant data that violated study guidelines during the data
collection phase.

During the second summer, I focused on revising the manuscript for publication by
expanding the Background & Related Work and Discussion sections to address reviewer
feedback. The paper was accepted to CSCW 2025 and includes one of my figures. I also
assisted with the literature review for a potential future publication for the lab.
