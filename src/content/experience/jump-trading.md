I benchmarked LLMs across tasks involving logic, spatial reasoning, and strategy-based
games, publishing the results in the two manuscripts linked below.

The first focused on building systems to optimize LLM play of 2048. We chose 2048 to
investigate a fairly simple, probabilistic game with elements of strategy, geometry, and
forward planning. For this paper I developed a recursive agent using Monte Carlo Tree
Search and LLM-based runtime code generation, improving game score by 5× over a random
baseline.

The second investigated LLMs' spatial reasoning more deeply — a follow-up question we
identified during the 2048 experiments.

Alongside the papers, I fine-tuned Llama and Qwen models on synthetic datasets to improve
model responses and behavior.
